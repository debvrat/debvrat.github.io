About Me
======
I develop machine learning-based computer vision algorithms for social good applications. Currently I work as a Postdoctoral Research Associate at Oak Ridge National Laboratory in Tennesse, USA, where I build diffusion models for synthesizing remote sensing images. I completed my Ph.D. from University of Maryland Baltimore County, M.Sc. from University of Twente, and B.E. from BITS Pilani Goa. During grad school, I interned at Microsoft Research, and have been a student researcher at Frontier Development Lab (SETI Institute), NASA JPL, and ISRO.
